# Lorestep v2 "Plotline" redesign plan

Branch `feat/design-v2`. Design only: no schema, API, auth or data change, no new feature, no new npm dependency. Look reference: `docs/design/v2-reference.html:57-73` (tokens, type-role rule) and its section compositions. No local builds or tests; CI runs `npm run check:ci`, the `extension` job and Playwright. Every step ends with one commit; a milestone ends CI-green.
Commit subjects: Conventional Commits per `CONTRIBUTING.md:70` (`feat(design): ...`). CONFLICT: `.claude/CLAUDE.md` section 6 asks for `feat/kebab` prefixes; CONTRIBUTING wins here because the owner named it. Spec conflicts, owner decision wins (recorded in the theme-file comment, DES-19): dark only (DES-03/24), uppercase display headings (DES-18), three font families (DES-D09).
Continuity: `TECH-STACK.md` and `DIRECTORY-STRUCTURE.md` do not exist at the repo root (glob), so nothing to update; `DRIFT.md` absent. The last step of each milestone also rewrites `STATE.md` in place (parallel steps never touch it). `DECISIONS.md` entries use Context/Options/Choice/Consequence/Revisit-when.

Lint traps: Stylelint standard: `(width <= 900px)` range media syntax, `#fff` short hex, `/ 62%` alpha, kebab-case classes/keyframes/custom properties. ESLint react-hooks v7: no synchronous `setState` in effects (use `useSyncExternalStore` for the mounted flag). Biome a11y: `type="button"`, `noNoninteractiveTabindex` (so no scroll container with tabindex: tables reflow instead of scrolling), SVG `aria-hidden`. Extension CSS is outside Stylelint and ESLint but inside Biome (120 cols).

## C. Demo picks for the story/release switch (games have years; most screen entries have `year: null`)
Metric: sum of rank displacement between story `order` and release rank (year, ties broken by story order), per `src/data/franchises.json`. Metal Gear `id: metal-gear`, 11 games, 50 (mean 4.5; its `caveat` says "nearly the reverse of release order"); Metroid `metroid`, 11 games, 32 (mean 2.9; Prime 4 is story 6 yet 2025, Metroid II is story 7); Ace Attorney `ace-attorney`, 10 games, 30 (mean 3.0; The Great Ace Attorney, 2015, is story 1). Runner-up Kingdom Hearts 20. Computed by hand from the JSON, not searched across all 126 franchises (no local script allowed); the ids are fixed in `DEMO_FRANCHISE_IDS`. Same-year games keep story order, and the foot line says so.
